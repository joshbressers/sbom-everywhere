# OSSF SBOM Community Working Group

Home of the OpenSSF SBOM Community Working Group. We're glad you're here!

Anyone is welcome to participate in this working group. You do not need to be an OpenSSF member to participate.

## Mission

The OpenSSF SBOM Community Working Group mission is to bring together the global SBOM community to further the creation, usage, and understanding of SBOMs. We accomplish this by leveraging open source principals to bring people together and encourage cooperation.

## Vision Statement

To make SBOMs accessible to everyone.

## Scope

The primary goal of the WG is to foster and grow the global SBOM community. We accomplish this by
- Bringing together and fostering cooperation of the global community
- Creating and publishing guidance to help educate and further adoption
- Maintaining open source SBOM tooling
